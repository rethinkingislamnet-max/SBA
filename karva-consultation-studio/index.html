Frequently asked questions

Is Karva a ladies and Muslimah friendly salon?

Karva describes its ladies salon as Muslimah and hijab friendly. Its hair and scalp service page also mentions wudhu friendly services and private rooms subject to availability. Confirm requirements with the outlet.

Will the suggested service definitely suit my hair?

No. The recommendations here are service category suggestions only. Karva’s professionals should assess your hair and discuss suitability and final prices.

Is a booking confirmed when I click WhatsApp?

No. WhatsApp opens a draft enquiry. Karva must respond and confirm availability separately.

Can I walk in?

Karva’s FAQ says walk ins are welcome, but booking in advance is recommended to avoid waiting.

Does this demo collect personal information?

No. Selections remain in this page only, and no account, database or tracking API is connected. WhatsApp is opened only if you choose to send an enquiry.
